01 / THE STORY

FROM A DREAM IN DELHI

NOT GIVEN.
EARNED.

Some players play the game. Some change the way a generation feels about it.

Born in Delhi on 5 November 1988, Virat Kohli grew from a determined young cricketer into one of the defining batters of modern cricket. His journey began in the streets and grounds of Delhi, continued through India's Under-19 triumph in 2008, and reached the international stage in the same year.

His story is built on extraordinary consistency, intense preparation, fearless run chases and an unmistakable desire to compete. He made number 18 a symbol recognised by cricket fans around the world.

THE STORY
IS STILL BEING WRITTEN.

Virat Kohli continues to represent India in ODI cricket and plays for Royal Challengers Bengaluru in the IPL. He retired from T20 internationals in 2024 and Test cricket in 2025.

His stated target: the 2027 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup, which he has described as his last World Cup for India.

04 / THE JOURNEY

EVERY ERA BUILT A NEW CHAPTER

THE ROAD TO
NUMBER 18.

2002
THE BEGINNING

Delhi's young prospect

Kohli began his journey in competitive cricket through Delhi's age-group system and developed into a promising young batter.

2008
THE BREAKTHROUGH

Under-19 World Cup champion

Captained India to the ICC Under-19 World Cup title in Malaysia. Made his senior ODI debut against Sri Lanka on 18 August 2008.

2011
WORLD CHAMPION

First senior World Cup

Became part of India's 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup-winning team. Made his Test debut later that year against the West Indies.

2012
THE CHASE MASTER

183 against Pakistan

Scored a remarkable 183 off 148 balls in the Asia Cup against Pakistan, announcing his ability to dominate major run chases.

2013
ANOTHER ICC TITLE

Champions Trophy winner

Helped India win the ICC Champions Trophy in England.

2014–17
LEADERSHIP ERA

Captaincy and record-breaking seasons

Became India's Test captain in December 2014. Produced outstanding batting seasons across formats, including 973 IPL runs in 2016.

2018
INDIVIDUAL EXCELLENCE

ICC's leading men's cricketer

Won the ICC Men's Cricketer of the Year, Test Cricketer of the Year and ODI Cricketer of the Year awards.

2022
THE MELBOURNE NIGHT

A T20 World Cup classic

Played an unforgettable unbeaten 82 against Pakistan at the Melbourne Cricket Ground.

2023
REWRITING HISTORY

50 ODI centuries

Scored 765 runs at the 2023 ODI World Cup, including three centuries. Became the first batter to reach 50 ODI centuries.

2024
WORLD CHAMPION AGAIN

T20 World Cup victory

Scored 76 in the final against South Africa and received Player of the Match. Announced his retirement from T20 internationals.

2025
NEW TROPHIES

Champions Trophy and IPL glory

Won the ICC Champions Trophy with India and celebrated his first IPL title with Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Retired from Test cricket in May.

2026
THE NEXT CHAPTER

Eyes on the 2027 World Cup

Continued in ODI cricket. Kohli said the 2027 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup would be his last World Cup for India.
